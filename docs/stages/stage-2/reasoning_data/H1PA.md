## 5. The top-up (only missing, unused tasks; nothing regenerated)

**How it is allocated.** The gap is split over the ten cells in proportion to
each cell's share of the existing pool. Then, per cell:
- tasks are sized by the measured acceptance and mean size, with P1's planning
  rule (1.5× oversupply or a 90% binomial chance, whichever is larger);
- tasks are picked round-robin over the cell's unused tasks' groups, P1's own
  rule.

**What it needs.**
- **115 tasks**, against an expected yield of about 311k supervised tokens; every
  cell has enough unused tasks.
- The selected tasks pass the prompt-level isolation rules. R0–R9 run again on
  the accepted units with their teacher text.

**How it runs.** P1's frozen generation protocol: the same teacher, sampling,
allowance and verifiers, with two candidates where the second runs only when
the first is rejected. Then P1v verification and the packer at $0.

**Cost** (P1's measured A100 session at $1.79/h): about 33 min, **$1.01 expected,
$2.60 hard cap**. Running it on L40S instead would first need L40S decode speed
measured.

**Optional fluid validation reserve.** 185 tasks, expected about 516k, would
cover the gap plus a 150k reserve, at the same session bound. Trajectories
beyond the 2.96M cut are never trained. They could feed a diagnostic fluid
validation set through the trainer's `extra_val` for both D4 arms.

## 7. Evaluation

[`nucleus_v1`](../../../../configs/shared/evaluation/decoding/nucleus_v1.json):
- **Sampling:** temperature 0.6, top-p 0.95, top-k and min-p disabled, no
  penalties; one sample per item.
- **Seeds:** per item and turn, independent of the model. Every checkpoint draws
  the same seed for the same item.
- **Records:** every sample keeps its seed, output token ids and `stop_reason`
  (eos / degeneration / context_limit).
- **Where it runs:** wired opt-in into `uncapped_eval.py`, `run_battery.py` and
  `evaluate_all`. Greedy calls are unchanged. A real vLLM check belongs to the GPU
  validation session.

**Primary measure:** correctness on the formal static items of
`fluid_reasoning_720` (held-out rule families), each axis apart, with every other
unit reported beside it.

**Isolation of E1's data against eval-set-1:**
- no fluid overlap;
- 9 near-copies in `eval_behavior_v0` and multihop, reported with that caveat;
- `stage3_legacy_150` is in-sample only.

No greedy result, E1's or H1's, is compared with a nucleus one.

## 9. Risks

- **Floor.** Under greedy decoding, E1's PCA 2.96M student answered 1.75% of the
  static fluid items, below the 2% floor H1's rule used. Comparison B may resolve
  only on deduction.
- **Run-to-run variance.** Two seeds per arm. GPU training is not bitwise
  deterministic (H1.md §16).
- **Different packing in the two parts.** E1's part is truncated, the increment
  is not. This is recorded, not hidden.
- **Durability.** PCA-Original seed 20260801 has one copy until it is uploaded.
