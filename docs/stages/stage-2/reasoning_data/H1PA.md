## 3. D4-Augmented's data: E1's pack unchanged, plus the fluid increment

- the fluid part is byte-identical to the fluid pack.

The merged set has its own identity, recorded when the complete increment is
built.

**The increment.**
- Only the five P10.2 primary axes.
- Verified teacher-native trajectories from P1 and P1t.
- No supporting data and no @v3 quota.
- Its mixture is the pool's own supervised-token composition, declared before
  packing.
- Packed by E1's sequential packer **without terminal truncation**
  (`--no-terminal-truncation`, the packer's own `allow_terminal_truncation=False`).

**Recorded processing difference:**
- E1's part keeps E1's terminal truncation: in its 2.96M cut, 352 sessions are
  truncated, 307 of them losing the answer entirely.
- The increment packs every verified trajectory whole.

**How the packer really groups** (pre-generation review, `audit/h1pa_pregen_review.json`):
- **Code.**
  - `pack_sessions` groups by `system_group_key` (the rendered system text plus
    tool schemas) and nothing else.
  - Category and source only order sessions (`interleave`) and sequences
    (`order_blocks`), and are recorded. No category or source boundary exists.
  - A unit's turns never share a sequence, so no turn reads another turn's
    answer.
- **The fluid increment:**
  - 1 system-prompt group;
  - 191 of 492 sequences hold two or more reasoning axes (202 two or more
    cells, 80 two or more sources), and every pair of axes shares sequences;
  - 1.49 samples per sequence (286 hold 1, 174 hold 2, 31 hold 3, 1 holds 4);
  - padding 25.8%; 0 sequences hold one unit twice;
  - 731/731 samples packed whole; 731/731 members traceable to task, source,
    axis and unit.
- **Why padding is higher than best-fit's.** Sequential packing closes a sequence
  when the next whole sample does not fit. It never looks ahead or reorders for
  fill.
- **E1's pack** (for reference): 1,312 system-prompt groups in its 2.96M cut,
  because tool schemas render into the system prompt. Its 465 default-prompt
  sequences mix types in 464 of them, at 6.27 samples per sequence and 2.0%
  padding.
- **Joint re-packing of E1 and the increment: not done.**
  - Only E1's default-prompt sessions could share with the fluid data.
  - At most 127 sequences (191 optimizer steps) could be saved.
  - It would end the property that D4-Augmented's E1 part is E1's pack, so
    Comparison B's attribution would blur.

## 5. The top-up (only missing, unused tasks; nothing regenerated)

**How it is allocated.** The gap is split over the ten cells in proportion to
each cell's share of the existing pool. Then, per cell:
- tasks are sized by the measured acceptance and mean size, with P1's planning
  rule (1.5× oversupply or a 90% binomial chance, whichever is larger);
- tasks are picked round-robin over the cell's unused tasks' groups, P1's own
  rule.

**What it needs.**
- **115 tasks**, against an expected yield of about 311k supervised tokens; every
  cell has enough unused tasks.
- The selected tasks pass the prompt-level isolation rules. R0–R9 run again on
  the accepted units with their teacher text.

**How it runs.** P1's frozen generation protocol: the same teacher, sampling,
allowance and verifiers, with two candidates where the second runs only when
the first is rejected. Then P1v verification and the packer at $0.

**Cost, from the session's own tasks**
([`plans/h1pa_topup_application.json`](../../../../logs/stages/stage-2/reasoning_data/plans/h1pa_topup_application.json)).

- **Model.** Each pass is processor sharing over its requests' generated tokens.
  An interactive episode counts as one request of all its turns.
- **Calibrated on P1's and P1t's four passes:**
  - aggregate throughput 2,049 tok/s (1,680 conservative);
  - single-sequence speed cap 45.9 tok/s;
  - ×1.321 for the largest under-prediction of a measured pass.
- **Fixed phases** (setup, contract check, two engine starts, teardown) are
  P1t's measured ones. The hard cap follows P1's rule.

### 12.1 The fluid increment, admitted by whole task units

**What was wrong.** The PA1 build cut the sequential pack at the first
sequences reaching 2.96M. That cut fell inside 4 multi-turn episodes: their
early turns were trained and their later turns were not. One turn being whole
does not make its episode whole.

**The rule now** (`h1pa_audit sessions --whole-units`, `admit_whole_units`):
- Take the packer's own session order: `build_token_ladder.py`'s seed-free
  stratified interleave on the declared mixture, rendered by the packer's
  `render_session` and tokenizer.
- Walk units in the order of their first session. Admit each unit with ALL
  its sessions until the admitted supervised tokens reach 2,960,000. The stop
  falls on a unit boundary.
- Pack exactly those units with E1's sequential packer (no truncation, no
  best-fit) and train every sequence.
- Nothing reads a model result, a correctness score or a length.

### 12.4 The driver, the launcher and durability

- **Driver.** [`h1pa_driver.py`](../../../../scripts/stages/stage-2/reasoning_data/h1pa_driver.py)
  reuses `unit_driver`'s state, resume, retry and terminal path.
  - **PCA-Original is evaluated only.** Its checkpoint is re-identified by
    sha256 and never retrained or persisted again.
  - **D4-Original** runs the seed's E1 config with only where-it-lives keys and
    `tokenizer_source` replaced.
  - **D4-Augmented** additionally takes E1's builder schedule from the merged
    set's real sequence count, checked against the study. Loss, optimizer,
    learning rate, batch size and micro-batch size are unchanged; tests compare
    every nested key.
  - **Decoding.** Every evaluation passes `nucleus_v1` explicitly. A unit
    refuses, and never falls back to greedy, when the protocol is missing, when
    generations on the volume were made under another protocol, or when a
    summary lacks it.
  - **Resume.** An interrupted training continues from its last save point
    with `--resume latest`. A trained unit resumes at scoring, and a scored one
    is skipped.
- **Launcher and governance.** [`h1pa_launch.py`](../../../../scripts/stages/stage-2/reasoning_data/h1pa_launch.py),
  [`h1pa_governance.py`](../../../../scripts/stages/stage-2/reasoning_data/h1pa_governance.py)

    (PA2), bound to the study identity and the harness, inside one cumulative
    envelope.
  - **Inputs.** A pod pulls only what its units read, hash-pinned from the relay
